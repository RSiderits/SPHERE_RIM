Sphere Rim Volume Calculator

This tool estimates the proportion of a roughly spherical tumor occupied by a staining rim. Enter the outer tumor radius and the radius from the center to the inner boundary of the staining rim. The calculator reports absolute volumes in mm³, the rim thickness in mm, the percent of total tumor volume represented by the rim, and displays a live diagram of the sphere and rim.

Calculation Summary
Parameter Value Meaning
Outer radius — Radius from center to tumor edge
Inner radius — Radius from center to inner rim boundary
Rim thickness — Outer radius minus inner radius
Total tumor volume — Entire spherical lesion volume
Inner core volume — Estimated central non-rim volume
Rim volume — Outer shell volume = total minus inner core
Rim fraction — Rim volume as a percentage of total tumor volume
Volumes are calculated as: V = 4/3 × π × r³.
Rim volume = outer sphere volume − inner sphere volume.
